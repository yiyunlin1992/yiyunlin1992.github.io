## About

<!-- Bio drafted from her CV and publications -- Yiyun should check the wording. -->
I am a postdoctoral associate in the [Landau Lab](https://landaulab.org/) at Weill Cornell Medicine and the New York Genome Center. I develop single-cell multi-omic approaches that read a cell's genome together with its transcriptome and chromatin, to study how somatic mutations evolve within cell lineages of normal tissue and how precancerous lesions progress to cancer.

I did my PhD in [Nicholas Navin's lab](https://navinlabcode.github.io/) at the MD Anderson Cancer Center UTHealth Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences, where I used single-cell DNA and RNA sequencing and spatial transcriptomics to discover rare aneuploid epithelial cells in normal breast tissue and to chart how triple-negative breast cancers respond to chemotherapy. Before that, I trained in pharmacy at Sichuan University, working on cancer metabolism and lipidomics.
